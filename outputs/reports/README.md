# Historical single-run reports and original tuning

These artifacts preserve earlier experiments. Quote final repeated-run comparisons from [the current shared summaries](../shared/model_comparison_aggregate.json) and [full report](../shared/report/full_report_draft.md). Original tuning CSVs remain inputs for explicit `--tuning-source legacy` extraction; choosing that source preserves both the legacy and canonical tables.

Historical metadata lacks the complete identities recorded by the refreshed pipeline. Its nominal seed labels do not establish independent initialization, and earlier loss histories retain their original reduction behavior. The files are retained for reference without retroactive provenance claims.
