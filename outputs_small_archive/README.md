# Historical experiment archive

This directory preserves earlier experiment artifacts. Current final comparisons are in [outputs/shared](../outputs/shared/model_comparison_aggregate.json), with model-specific runs and aggregates under `outputs/<model>/`.

These archived results have a separate experiment scope. Their metadata does not establish the complete source, data, preprocessing, or effective seed identities recorded by current runs. They remain historical reference material and are excluded from current aggregation and analysis.
